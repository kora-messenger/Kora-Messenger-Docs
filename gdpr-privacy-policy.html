2. Personal Data Categories Collected

We process only minimal personal data necessary to provide our privacy-first end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) messaging service. The categories of personal data collected include:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, full name, chosen username, cryptographic password hash (Argon2/salted hash), and optional phone number.
  • Voice Vector Matrix: High-dimensional floating-point numerical vectors representing pitch, timbre, and acoustic resonance extracted locally on-device. Raw audio recordings are never stored on or transmitted to Kora servers.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: Mobile device identifiers (e.g. Android ID, iOS identifierForVendor), operating system version, Flutter application version, and connection IP address (processed transiently for network routing and security auditing).
  • Billing & Subscription Data: Payment transaction metadata for Premium subscriptions processed securely via Paystack. Kora does not store raw payment card numbers.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (Art. 6 GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases established in Article 6(1) of the GDPR:

  • Performance of a Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing email, username, password hash, and E2EE key registration data is necessary to create your account and deliver core messaging services under our Terms of Service.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Processing optional phone numbers, enabling Voice Vector Matrix cloud synchronization, and activating camera-based face and hand visual effects rely on your explicit consent, which can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing IP addresses, rate-limiting metadata, and device info is based on our legitimate interest in securing our infrastructure, preventing abuse, detecting DDoS attacks, and ensuring platform reliability.
  • Legal Obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)): Retaining financial transaction logs processed through Paystack as required by statutory accounting, tax, and anti-money laundering laws.

4. Special Categories of Data (Art. 9 GDPR)

Kora Messenger strictly does NOT process special categories of personal data as defined under Article 9(1) GDPR (e.g., data revealing racial origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, genetic data, or health data).

Voice Vector Matrix Clarification: The mathematical vectors generated by Kora's Voice Vector Matrix are used exclusively for real-time speech synthesis during live translated calls. They are NOT used for biometric identification or authentication, do not uniquely identify an individual in a biometric system, and do not constitute special category biometric data under Article 9 GDPR.

5. Data Subject Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you hold the following statutory rights under Chapter III of the GDPR:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Request confirmation as to whether your personal data is being processed and obtain a copy of your stored data.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data via in-app profile settings or contacting our DPO.
  • Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17): Request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): Request that Kora suspend processing your personal data under specific statutory conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (JSON/CSV).
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests at any time.
  • Right Not to be Subject to Automated Decision-Making (Art. 22): Right not to be evaluated by automated processing or profiling that produces legal or significant effects.

6. Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7)

Where processing is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), you have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. You can withdraw consent by toggling optional features off in Kora app settings or by emailing dpo@koramessenger.com.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to the withdrawal.

7. Right to Lodge a Complaint with a Supervisory Authority (Art. 77)

If you believe that Kora's processing of your personal data infringes the GDPR, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in the EU Member State of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.

8. Data Retention Periods

We adhere to strict data minimization and retention policies:

  • Account Credentials & Profile: Retained while your account is active. Permanently purged within 30 days of account deletion.
  • E2EE Message Blobs: Messages are delivered using end-to-end encryption. Undelivered encrypted message blobs are held on servers for up to 30 days maximum, after which they are permanently deleted. Delivered messages reside only on sender and recipient devices.
  • Voice Vector Matrix: Stored in encrypted form while active; permanently deleted immediately upon account deletion.
  • Server Logs & IP Addresses: Network connection logs and IP addresses are retained for up to 30 days for security auditing and rate-limiting, then automatically purged or anonymized.
  • Payment Records: Paystack billing records retained for 5 to 7 years as mandated by statutory financial regulations.

9. Cross-Border Data Transfers and Safeguards (Chapter V)

When personal data originating from the European Economic Area (EEA) is transferred to servers outside the EEA, Kora ensures full compliance with Chapter V of the GDPR by implementing standard contractual clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission or relying on adequacy decisions.

10. Automated Processing and Profiling

Kora Messenger does NOT engage in automated decision-making, automated profiling, or behavioral scoring having legal or similarly significant effects under Article 22 GDPR.

11. Children's Data (Art. 8)

Kora Messenger is intended for users aged 16 and older in the European Union. Where member state law permits lower age thresholds (down to 13) with verified parental consent under Article 8 GDPR, Kora requires parental approval. Accounts found to belong to children under the applicable legal age threshold without parental consent will be promptly terminated.

12. Technical and Organizational Data Security Measures (Art. 32)

In accordance with Article 32 GDPR, Kora implements robust security controls:

  • End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): X3DH key agreement protocol, Double Ratchet algorithm, and AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption for all user communications.
  • On-Device Computing: ML Kit, CoreML, and facial landmark processing operate strictly on-device.
  • Transit & Rest Security: TLS 1.3 protocol for all network transport and AES-256 database encryption at rest.

13. Personal Data Breach Notification (Arts. 33-34)

In the event of a personal data breach, Kora will notify the relevant supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, not later than 72 hours after becoming aware of it (Art. 33). If the breach is likely to result in a high risk to data subjects' rights and freedoms, Kora will notify affected users without undue delay (Art. 34).

14. International Transfers Outside EEA

All international transfers of EEA personal data strictly utilize European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), supplement technical encryption controls, and ensure equivalent data protection standards.

15. Data Protection by Design and Default (Art. 25)

Privacy is embedded into Kora's core engineering. End-to-end encryption is enabled by default for all private chats, local processing is prioritized over cloud computing, and zero-data-retention mechanisms are applied across interactive features.

16. Updates to This GDPR Privacy Policy

We may update this GDPR Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal requirements or platform updates. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notifications or registered email.

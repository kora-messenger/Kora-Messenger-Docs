Supplemental Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Feature-Specific Addenda to Main Terms & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

These Supplemental Terms of Service & Privacy Policy ("Supplemental Terms") govern your use of specific advanced features provided within Kora Messenger. These terms supplement and form an integral part of the main Kora Messenger Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

1. Voice Vector Matrix Supplemental Terms

Kora Messenger offers a Voice Vector Matrix feature that enables high-fidelity voice-style synthesis during live translated audio calls.

  • On-Device Generation: Voice vectors consist of mathematical floating-point numerical matrices representing vocal resonance and pitch characteristics. Vectors are extracted entirely on your device using local DSP modules. Raw voice recordings are never created, captured, or transmitted.
  • Encrypted Server Storage: Your mathematical voice vector is encrypted with your account key and stored on Kora servers solely to enable cross-device sync and live call synthesis with authorized contacts.
  • Authorized Sharing: Voice vectors are transmitted only to contacts with whom you initiate a live translated call.
  • Account Deletion: Your stored voice vector matrix is permanently deleted from Kora servers upon account deletion.
  • Non-Reconstructible & Non-Biometric: The voice vector matrix is mathematically one-way and cannot be used to reconstruct your original spoken audio. It is not used for biometric identification or identity verification.

2. On-Device Translation Supplemental Terms

Kora Messenger integrates local machine learning frameworks—Google ML Kit on Android and Apple Translation API on iOS—to power text message translation.

  • On-Demand Pack Downloads: Language translation models are downloaded on-demand directly to your device storage.
  • Zero Server Plaintext Transmission: Translation runs 100% locally on your device hardware. Unencrypted plaintext messages are never sent to Kora servers or external third-party translation endpoints.
  • Accuracy Disclaimer: On-device automated translation is provided "as is". Kora does not guarantee perfect translation accuracy or contextual precision.

3. Live Call Translation Supplemental Terms

During real-time voice calls, Kora provides on-device speech translation and synthesis.

  • Local DSP Processing: Speech-to-text recognition and text translation occur locally on the calling device using Digital Signal Processing (DSP) and local models.
  • WebRTC Data Channel Transmission: WebRTC encrypted data channels transmit ONLY translated text strings (and synthesized audio frames), NOT raw call audio.
  • Zero-Data Retention (ZDR): Synthesis parameters and transient audio packets operate under strict Zero-Data Retention (ZDR) and are discarded immediately after playback.

Kora Messenger includes an integrated assistant, Kora AI, powered by AI models.

  • Free Access: Kora AI is accessible free of charge to all registered Kora Messenger users.
  • Informational Use Only: AI responses are generated automatically for informational and conversational purposes. They do not constitute professional, legal, medical, or financial advice.
  • Accuracy Disclaimer: AI responses may occasionally be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. Users should independently verify critical information.

5. Multi-Device Sync Supplemental Terms

Kora supports multi-device message and key synchronization.

  • Device Identifiers: To maintain secure sessions, Kora uses platform-standard non-persistent device identifiers (ANDROID_ID on Android and identifierForVendor on iOS).
  • Trusted Device Management: You can review, approve, and revoke active sessions and trusted devices at any time in Kora Settings.

6. Web Pairing Supplemental Terms

Kora Messenger allows users to pair web clients via /api/v1/web/pair.

  • Cryptographic Handshake: Web pairing requires a secure QR code scan and cryptographic key exchange between your mobile primary device and the browser session.
  • Session Security: Paired web sessions route messages through end-to-end encrypted session keys established directly with your mobile device.

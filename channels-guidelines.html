Kora Messenger Channels Guidelines

Rules & Governance for Channel Creators, Admins, and Subscribers

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

1. What Are Kora Channels?

Kora Channels is a one-to-many broadcasting feature that empowers creators, public figures, news organizations, non-profits, businesses, and communities to broadcast messages, media, announcements, and updates directly to subscriber audiences.

2. Channel Creation Standards

Any registered Kora Messenger user in good standing may create a channel. Channel creation must comply with the following criteria:

  • Channel Naming: Names must be accurate and non-misleading. Impersonation of brands, public figures, or other organizations is strictly prohibited.
  • Descriptions & Bio: Channel descriptions must clearly reflect the nature and content of the channel.
  • Avatars & Branding: Profile images and banners must adhere to community standards and must not infringe third-party copyrights or trademarks.

3. Channel Admin Responsibilities

Channel administrators bear primary responsibility for maintaining their channel environment:

  • Active Moderation: Admins must actively monitor subscriber interactions (such as comments or reactions) and swiftly remove abusive content.
  • Compliance: Admins must ensure all channel posts comply with Kora's Community Guidelines, Terms of Service, and Channel Guidelines.
  • Admin Management: Main owners are responsible for actions taken by appointed co-admins.

4. Channel Content Standards

All content broadcast through Kora Channels must adhere to our high standards of safety and integrity. Prohibited content includes:

  • Illegal activities, dangerous organizations, and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Hate speech, harassment, bullying, and targeted harassment of individuals.
  • Deceptive scams, phishing, financial fraud, malware distribution, or bulk unsolicited spam.
  • Misleading medical disinformation or fraudulent financial schemes.

5. Channel Discovery and Directory

Public channels may be featured in Kora's searchable Channel Directory. To qualify for inclusion in search results and discovery lists, channels must maintain active posting, comply with content policies, and avoid policy violations.

6. Channel Engagement Features

Channels support interactive audience engagement tools:

  • Emoji Reactions: Subscribers can express feedback using custom emoji reactions.
  • Subscriber Comments: Where enabled by admins, comments allow discussion under channel posts.
  • Polls & Surveys: Interactive polls enable real-time audience feedback.

7. Channel Verification

Official channels representing recognized public figures, media outlets, global brands, or verified organizations may apply for a Kora Verification Badge (Blue Checkmark). Verification authenticates identity and prevents impersonation.

8. Channel Privacy Safeguards

Privacy is fundamental to Kora Channels:

  • Subscriber Anonymity: Other channel subscribers cannot view your personal profile, full name, username, or phone number.
  • Admin View Restrictions: Channel admins cannot view individual subscriber identity lists or phone numbers; they receive only anonymized, aggregated subscriber counts and engagement metrics.

9. Channel Monetization (Future Framework)

Kora is developing a monetization framework (such as premium channels, subscriber support, or sponsored broadcasts). All future monetization features will operate under transparent publisher agreements and regional financial compliance regulations.

10. Channel Reporting and Enforcement

Users can report channels or specific broadcasts directly within the app. Kora enforces policy compliance using progressive measures:

  • Content warning or removal of infringing posts.
  • Removal from the public Channel Directory and search index.
  • Temporary or permanent channel suspension.
  • Account-level bans for severe or repeated violations.

11. Channel Transfer and Ownership

Channel owners may transfer administrative control to another appointed admin. Channels may be permanently deleted by the owner at any time, which purges channel history and subscriber associations.

12. Channel Restrictions

Channels containing mature themes permitted under policy must apply age-restricted tags (18+). Kora may also restrict channel availability in specific jurisdictions to comply with local legal requirements.

13. Relationship to Other Policies

These Guidelines supplement Kora's Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Community Guidelines, and AI Policy.

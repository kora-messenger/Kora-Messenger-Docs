AI Policy

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Kora Messenger uses artificial intelligence ("AI") to provide communication, translation, transcription, assistance, safety, and other features.

This AI Policy explains how Kora's AI features are intended to work, what users can expect from them, and what users are responsible for when using them.

This Policy applies to Kora AI Assistant and Kora's AI-powered translation, transcription, voice, video, calling, and other AI features.

1. Kora AI Assistant

Kora may provide an integrated Kora AI Assistant that users can interact with through text, voice, images, files, or other supported methods.

Kora AI may help with:

  • Answering questions
  • Writing and rewriting
  • Summarizing information
  • Translation
  • Language assistance
  • Brainstorming
  • General information
  • Voice-related features
  • Other supported tasks

The capabilities of Kora AI may change as the service develops.

2. AI-Generated Responses

Kora AI generates responses automatically.

AI-generated information may sometimes be:

  • Incorrect
  • Incomplete
  • Outdated
  • Misleading
  • Missing important context

You should independently verify important information before relying on it.

Kora AI should not be treated as a guaranteed source of factual, professional, or expert advice.

3. High-Risk Decisions

Do not rely solely on Kora AI for decisions involving:

  • Medical treatment
  • Emergency situations
  • Legal matters
  • Financial decisions
  • Personal safety
  • Other high-risk situations

When appropriate, consult a qualified professional or trusted source.

4. AI Translation

Kora may use AI and automated language-processing technology to translate:

  • Text messages
  • Group messages
  • Voice messages
  • Voice transcripts
  • Status content
  • Channel content
  • Captions
  • Calls
  • Video calls
  • Other supported content

Translation is intended to help users communicate across languages.

5. Translation Accuracy

AI translation may not always be accurate.

Errors may occur because of:

  • Slang
  • Local expressions
  • Accents
  • Background noise
  • Poor audio quality
  • Multiple speakers
  • Context
  • Ambiguous language
  • Unsupported language variations

For important conversations, users should verify the original message where necessary.

6. Voice-Note Translation

Kora may allow users to translate voice messages.

The process may involve:

Voice Message → Speech Recognition → Text → Translation → Translated Text and/or Voice

Depending on the available feature, Kora may provide:

  • Original transcript
  • Translated transcript
  • Translated audio
  • Language selection
  • Automatic language detection

7. Voice Transcription

Kora may convert spoken audio into written text.

Transcription may be used for:

  • Voice messages
  • Calls
  • Video calls
  • Captions
  • Translation
  • Other supported features

Transcriptions are generated automatically and may contain mistakes.

8. Live Call Translation

Kora may provide live translation during:

  • One-to-one audio calls
  • One-to-one video calls
  • Group audio calls
  • Group video calls

The system may process speech in real time to provide:

  • Translated speech
  • Live captions
  • Translated captions
  • Translated audio

9. Group Call Translation

Kora's live translation features may work with multiple participants.

The system may process speech from different participants and attempt to:

  • Identify speech
  • Separate speakers where technically possible
  • Detect languages
  • Translate speech
  • Display captions
  • Provide translated audio

Because multiple people may speak simultaneously, translation accuracy may decrease.

10. Live Translation Limitations

Live translation may experience:

  • Delays
  • Incorrect translations
  • Missing words
  • Incorrect speaker detection
  • Language-detection errors
  • Audio interruptions
  • Network-related delays
  • Background-noise problems

Kora does not guarantee perfect real-time translation.

11. AI Captions

Kora may provide AI-generated captions for voice and video communication.

Captions may display:

  • Original speech
  • Translated speech
  • Both, depending on the selected feature

Captions may contain errors and should not be treated as an official transcript.

12. Uploaded Voice, Audio, and Video

Kora may allow users to upload audio or video from their device for supported AI features.

Examples may include:

  • Voice processing
  • Transcription
  • Translation
  • Voice-related AI features
  • Other supported functionality

The uploaded content may be processed by Kora or authorized technology providers necessary to provide the requested feature.

13. Voice and Video Consent

Kora takes voice and video consent seriously.

Before certain uploads, Kora may display a warning:

Don't upload any person's voice, audio, or video without their consent.

The user may then be required to confirm:

I agree that this is my video/audio or I agree I have asked the owner of the video/audio before uploading it.

Users must provide truthful confirmation.

14. Voice Ownership and Permission

Uploading someone's voice or video does not automatically give you the right to process or reproduce it.

Users are responsible for obtaining the appropriate permission or authorization before uploading another person's voice, audio, or video.

This includes using AI-powered features that transform, translate, reproduce, or otherwise process the media.

15. AI Voice Features

If Kora provides features that generate, transform, translate, or reproduce voice, users must not use them to:

  • Fraudulently impersonate another person
  • Scam another person
  • Defraud users
  • Create harmful deceptive content
  • Harass another person
  • Misrepresent another person's words
  • Create unauthorized reproductions of another person's voice

Additional restrictions may apply depending on the specific feature.

16. AI-Generated Content

AI may generate:

  • Text
  • Translations
  • Summaries
  • Captions
  • Audio
  • Other supported content

AI-generated content may not always represent verified facts or the views of Kora.

Users are responsible for how they use and share AI-generated content.

17. Misrepresentation of AI Content

Users should not intentionally use AI-generated content to create serious deception, fraud, impersonation, or harm.

Where a Kora feature clearly identifies content as AI-generated or AI-assisted, users should not deliberately remove or manipulate that indication for deceptive purposes where prohibited.

18. AI and Impersonation

Kora AI must not be used to impersonate another person for:

  • Fraud
  • Scams

  • Identity theft
  • Harassment
  • Extortion
  • Other harmful purposes

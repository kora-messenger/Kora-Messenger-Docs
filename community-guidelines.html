Community Guidelines

Effective Date: August 23, 2026

Last Updated: August 23, 2026

Kora Messenger is built to help people communicate, connect, share, and create. These Community Guidelines explain the behavior we expect from everyone using Kora.

They apply to private chats, group chats, voice messages, calls, Status, Channels, profiles, comments, media uploads, Kora AI interactions, and other Kora features.

1. Treat People With Respect

Kora should be a place where people can communicate without being subjected to abuse.

Do not use Kora to:

  • Harass or bully people
  • Threaten people
  • Intimidate people
  • Stalk people
  • Repeatedly target someone with unwanted communication
  • Encourage others to attack someone
  • Humiliate or deliberately degrade others

2. No Threats or Violence

You must not use Kora to make serious threats of violence or encourage violence against another person or group.

This includes:

  • Threatening to physically harm someone
  • Encouraging someone to harm another person
  • Coordinating violent attacks
  • Celebrating or encouraging real-world violence

Kora may take immediate action when there is a serious safety concern.

3. No Hate or Targeted Abuse

Kora does not allow content that attacks people based on protected characteristics or encourages hatred or violence against them.

This includes:

  • Dehumanizing people
  • Promoting violence against protected groups
  • Serious targeted hateful abuse
  • Encouraging discrimination or persecution

4. No Sexual Exploitation or Abuse

Kora prohibits sexual exploitation and abusive sexual content.

Users must not use Kora to:

  • Sexually exploit another person
  • Share intimate content without permission
  • Threaten to release intimate content
  • Blackmail someone using intimate content
  • Distribute sexual content involving minors
  • Facilitate sexual exploitation

5. Child Safety

Kora has a strict approach to protecting children.

You must never use Kora to:

  • Sexually exploit a child
  • Groom a child
  • Request sexual content from a child
  • Share sexual content involving a child
  • Encourage a child to participate in sexual activity
  • Facilitate child exploitation

Serious violations may result in immediate account action and reporting to appropriate authorities where required.

6. Respect Privacy

Do not use Kora to invade another person's privacy.

This includes:

  • Sharing someone's private information without appropriate authorization
  • Publishing private conversations maliciously
  • Sharing private photographs or videos without permission
  • Revealing sensitive personal information
  • Attempting to obtain another person's private account information

7. Voice, Audio and Video Consent

You must respect people's rights when uploading or processing their voice, audio, or video.

Before uploading another person's media, you should have the appropriate permission or authorization.

Kora may display a warning before certain uploads:

Don't upload any person's voice, audio, or video without their consent.

You may be required to confirm:

I agree that this is my video/audio or I agree I have asked the owner of the video/audio before uploading it.

Do not falsely confirm consent.

8. Do Not Impersonate Others

You must not pretend to be:

  • Another Kora user
  • A celebrity or public figure
  • A business
  • A government organization
  • Kora
  • Kora Support
  • Kora AI
  • Another official Kora account

Parody or clearly disclosed fan accounts may be treated differently where permitted.

9. Protect Against Scams and Fraud

Kora must not be used to deceive people for financial or personal gain.

Examples include:

  • Investment scams
  • Romance scams
  • Fake giveaways
  • Fake employment offers
  • Fake customer-support accounts
  • Payment scams
  • Phishing
  • Fake cryptocurrency schemes
  • Identity theft
  • Advance-fee scams
  • Fraudulent fundraising

10. No Phishing or Credential Theft

Do not use Kora to steal:

  • Passwords
  • Verification codes
  • Authentication information
  • Banking information
  • Payment credentials
  • Recovery codes
  • Other sensitive account information

Do not create links or messages designed to trick users into giving away their credentials.

11. Spam

Kora prohibits abusive spam.

Do not:

  • Send large numbers of unwanted messages
  • Repeatedly contact users who do not want communication
  • Create accounts solely for spam
  • Use automated systems to mass-message users without authorization
  • Repeatedly send promotional messages without appropriate permission
  • Manipulate engagement

12. Malware and Harmful Software

You must not use Kora to distribute:

  • Viruses
  • Malware
  • Spyware
  • Ransomware
  • Credential stealers
  • Malicious files
  • Other software designed to harm or compromise devices

Do not intentionally send files designed to compromise another person's device.

13. Hacking and Unauthorized Access

Do not use Kora to:

  • Hack accounts
  • Attempt to access another user's account
  • Circumvent security systems
  • Exploit vulnerabilities for malicious purposes
  • Attack Kora's infrastructure
  • Interfere with Kora's services
  • Steal authentication tokens
  • Bypass access controls

Security researchers should follow Kora's authorized vulnerability-reporting process where available.

14. Illegal Activities

Kora must not be used to facilitate serious unlawful activity.

This includes using Kora to organize, coordinate, or facilitate illegal activities where prohibited by applicable law.

15. Dangerous or Harmful Content

Kora may restrict content that creates a serious and immediate risk of harm.

This may include instructions or coordination intended to facilitate serious wrongdoing.

Educational, news, historical, artistic, or other legitimate contexts may be considered where appropriate.

16. Manipulation and Deception

Do not use Kora to deliberately manipulate people through deceptive practices that cause significant harm.

This includes:

  • Coordinated deception
  • Fake identities used for fraud
  • Fabricated evidence used to harm someone
  • Organized fraudulent campaigns
  • Deliberate impersonation

17. Misuse of AI

Kora AI must not be used to:

  • Facilitate fraud
  • Generate scams
  • Harass people
  • Create malicious impersonations
  • Generate harmful deceptive content
  • Facilitate unauthorized access
  • Create abusive spam
  • Circumvent Kora's safety systems

Additional requirements are provided in the Kora AI Policy.

18. AI-Generated Voice and Media

Kora may provide AI-powered voice, translation, or media features.

Users must not use these features to:

  • Impersonate someone fraudulently
  • Defraud people
  • Create harmful deceptive media
  • Misrepresent AI-generated content as authentic where doing so causes serious harm
  • Create unauthorized voice reproductions of another person

Where Kora requires consent confirmation for voice or media processing, users must provide truthful confirmation.

19. Translation Abuse

Translation features must not be used to facilitate abuse.

For example, users must not intentionally use translation tools to:

  • Threaten people
  • Harass people
  • Conduct scams
  • Spread prohibited content
  • Circumvent moderation

Translation does not make prohibited content acceptable.

20. Voice and Video Call Abuse

Users must not use Kora calls to:

  • Harass people
  • Threaten people
  • Conduct scams
  • Impersonate others
  • Record or distribute communications unlawfully
  • Facilitate exploitation

Users must follow applicable laws concerning recording and sharing communications.

21. Group Chat Responsibilities

Group administrators have additional responsibilities.

Administrators should:

  • Remove abusive users when necessary
  • Manage group permissions responsibly
  • Prevent serious abuse
  • Avoid using groups for prohibited activities

Group administrators should not intentionally create or maintain groups primarily designed for serious abuse or illegal activity.

22. Channel Responsibilities

Channel owners and administrators are responsible for the content they publish.

Channels must not be used primarily to distribute:

  • Scams
  • Malware
  • Serious abusive content
  • Illegal material
  • Exploitative content
  • Prohibited spam

Kora may take action against Channels that repeatedly violate these Guidelines.

23. Profiles and Usernames

Profiles and usernames must not be used to:

  • Impersonate others
  • Conduct scams
  • Promote serious abuse
  • Misrepresent official Kora accounts
  • Circumvent account restrictions

Kora may reserve or reclaim usernames when necessary.

24. Kora Support Impersonation

Users must not create accounts pretending to be Kora Support.

Be cautious if someone claiming to be Kora Support asks for:

  • Passwords
  • Verification codes
  • Recovery codes
  • Payment credentials

Official Kora Support will be identified through Kora's official verification system.

25. Verification Badges

Verification badges must not be manipulated or falsely represented.

Users must not:

  • Fake a Kora verification badge
  • Claim that an unofficial badge is official
  • Copy Kora's official identity
  • Use visual tricks designed to make an account appear officially verified

26. Premium Abuse

Kora Premium must not be used to:

  • Evade enforcement
  • Conduct scams
  • Impersonate official accounts
  • Spam users
  • Abuse Premium-only features

Premium status does not exempt an account from Community Guidelines.

27. Reporting

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these Guidelines, use Kora's reporting tools where available.

You may be able to report:

  • Users
  • Messages
  • Groups
  • Channels
  • Status
  • Profiles
  • Scams
  • Impersonation
  • Other harmful content

28. Do Not Abuse Reporting

Reports should be made honestly.

Do not:

  • Submit knowingly false reports
  • Mass-report someone to harass them
  • Use reporting systems to silence people unfairly
  • Coordinate fraudulent reports

Abuse of reporting systems may result in action against your account.

29. Blocking

If another user is bothering you, Kora may provide blocking tools.

Blocking may restrict communication such as:

  • Messages
  • Calls
  • Profile interactions

Users should use blocking and reporting tools when appropriate.

30. Enforcement

Kora may take action when these Guidelines are violated.

Depending on the circumstances, Kora may:

  • Remove content
  • Restrict content
  • Issue warnings
  • Restrict features
  • Limit messaging
  • Limit calls
  • Remove verification
  • Suspend accounts
  • Permanently disable accounts
  • Restrict Channels or groups
  • Take other appropriate measures

The severity of the action may depend on the seriousness and frequency of the violation.

31. Immediate Action

Kora may take immediate action when content or behavior presents a serious risk involving:

  • Child safety
  • Credible threats
  • Severe abuse
  • Fraud
  • Account security
  • Malware
  • Serious illegal activity
  • Other significant safety risks

32. Appeals

Where an appeal process is available, users may request a review of certain enforcement decisions.

Kora may review relevant information before making a decision.

Submitting an appeal does not guarantee that an account or content will be restored.

33. Automated and Human Review

Kora may use a combination of:

  • Automated detection
  • Security systems
  • User reports
  • Human review
  • Other moderation tools

to identify potential violations.

Automated systems can make mistakes.

Kora may provide review or appeal mechanisms where appropriate.

34. Privacy During Moderation

Kora will handle information used for moderation and security according to its Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Access to user information should be limited to legitimate purposes.

35. Context Matters

Kora may consider context when evaluating content.

Factors may include:

  • Educational purpose
  • News reporting
  • Documentary purpose
  • Satire
  • Artistic expression
  • Public-interest discussion
  • Self-defense or safety context

However, context does not automatically make prohibited conduct acceptable.

36. Repeated Violations

Repeated violations may result in stronger enforcement.

An account that repeatedly violates Kora's rules may lose access to certain features or the entire platform.

37. Evasion of Enforcement

Users must not create new accounts or use other methods to intentionally bypass a Kora restriction or suspension.

Attempts to evade enforcement may result in additional action.

38. Protecting the Kora Community

Everyone using Kora has a role in maintaining a safe community.

We encourage users to:

  • Treat others respectfully
  • Protect their accounts
  • Report suspicious activity
  • Avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily
  • Obtain consent before sharing other people's media
  • Think carefully before forwarding content
  • Verify suspicious information
  • Use privacy and security settings

39. Our Commitment

Kora will continue improving its safety systems as the platform develops.

We aim to balance:

  • Safety
  • Privacy
  • Free expression
  • Security
  • User control
  • Responsible innovation

40. Changes to These Guidelines

Kora may update these Community Guidelines as new features, technologies, threats, and community needs develop.

If significant changes are made, Kora may provide appropriate notice.

The latest version will always include the applicable Last Updated date.

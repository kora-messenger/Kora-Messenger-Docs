Cookie and Local Storage Policy for Kora Messenger

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Kora Messenger does not use tracking cookies to monitor your web activity or serve advertisements. However, to make our platform functional and private, we utilize local device storage (caches, device keys, and directories).

1. Cryptographic Data Storage

Your phone securely stores your private end-to-end encryption keys locally. Kora servers cannot see or access these keys. These keys are stored in your device's secure keystore (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. AI Translation Packs

When you request a dynamic translation, Kora downloads local AI language dictionaries directly to your device storage. This allows translation to execute instantly without relying on a network. These language packs:

  • Are downloaded on-demand only when you request a specific language
  • Are stored in your device's app-specific storage directory
  • Can be deleted at any time to free up space
  • Are never shared with other apps or uploaded to our servers

3. Conversation Databases

Your chat histories, sent media files, and local logs are stored directly on your phone's encrypted hardware. This data:

  • Never leaves your device in plaintext form
  • Is not backed up to Kora's servers (unless you explicitly enable encrypted backup)
  • Is permanently deleted when you clear a chat or delete your account

4. No Tracking Cookies

Kora Messenger does not use:

  • Tracking cookies or pixels
  • Advertising SDKs or ad networks
  • Third-party analytics that collect personal data
  • Cross-site tracking identifiers

5. Managing Your Local Storage

You can manage your local storage at any time:

  • Clear Chat History: Delete individual chats or all conversations from Kora's in-app settings
  • Delete Language Packs: Remove downloaded translation models from Settings → Translation
  • Delete Account: Permanently erase all account data, Voice Vector Matrix, and local caches
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling Kora removes all local data from your device

Blocking and Reporting

Effective Date: August 31, 2026 | Last Updated: August 31, 2026

Safety Tool

Kora Messenger provides blocking and reporting tools to help you control your experience and protect yourself from unwanted contact, abuse, or harmful content. This policy explains how blocking and reporting work, what happens when you use them, and how Kora handles your reports.

1. Blocking

Blocking is a tool that lets you stop another user from contacting you. When you block someone on Kora:

  • Messages: The blocked user cannot send you messages. Their messages will not be delivered to you.
  • Calls: The blocked user cannot call you (voice or video).
  • Status: The blocked user cannot see your Status updates, and you cannot see theirs.
  • Profile: The blocked user cannot see your profile photo, last seen, online status, or updates to your profile.
  • Groups: If you and a blocked user are in the same group, you will still receive each other's messages in that group. However, you can choose to exit the group.
  • Channels: Blocking does not prevent you from seeing a blocked user's public Channel posts if you follow the same Channel.

Blocking is private — the blocked user is not notified that they have been blocked. However, they may notice that they can no longer contact you or see your information.

You can unblock a user at any time through Settings → Privacy → Blocked Contacts.

2. Reporting

Reporting allows you to flag content or behavior that violates Kora's Terms of Service or Community Guidelines. You can report:

  • Users: Report a user's profile, behavior, or activity
  • Messages: Report a specific message in a private or group chat
  • Groups: Report a group for prohibited content or activity
  • Channels: Report a Channel for prohibited content
  • Status: Report a Status update
  • Scams: Report suspected fraud, phishing, or scams
  • Impersonation: Report an account pretending to be you or someone else
  • AI-Related Problems: Report harmful, inaccurate, or abusive AI-generated content

3. How to Report

To report content or a user in Kora Messenger:

  • Report a message: Long-press the message → Report
  • Report a user: Open the chat → tap the contact name → Report
  • Report a group: Open the group → tap the group name → Report
  • Report a Channel: Open the Channel → tap the Channel name → Report
  • Report a Status: Long-press the Status → Report

When you submit a report, Kora receives:

  • The reported content or message
  • The reported user's Kora ID and username
  • Your Kora ID and username (as the reporter)
  • The timestamp of the report
  • The reason for the report

Because Kora uses end-to-end encryption, Kora can only access the specific reported message when you submit the report. Kora cannot proactively read your messages.

4. What Kora Does with Reports

When you submit a report, Kora may:

  • Review the reported content
  • Investigate the reported user's account activity
  • Take enforcement action against the reported user (warnings, feature restrictions, suspension, or permanent disabling)
  • Remove prohibited content
  • Refer serious cases to law enforcement where required by law
  • Take no action if the report does not identify a policy violation

Kora may not be able to provide you with specific details about the outcome of a report due to privacy and safety considerations.

5. Reporting Honestly

Reports should be made honestly and in good faith. Do not:

  • Submit knowingly false reports
  • Mass-report someone to harass or silence them
  • Coordinate fraudulent or abusive reports with other users
  • Use reporting as a tool for retaliation or intimidation

Abuse of the reporting system may result in enforcement action against your own account.

6. Report Categories

When you report content, you can select a category to help Kora understand the issue:

  • Spam: Unsolicited mass messaging, promotional spam
  • Harassment or Bullying: Targeted abuse, threats, intimidation
  • Hate Speech: Attacks based on protected characteristics
  • Scam or Fraud: Phishing, financial scams, impersonation for financial gain
  • Illegal Content: Content that violates applicable law
  • Adult Content: Explicit or sexual content
  • Child Safety: Content involving the exploitation of minors
  • Misinformation: Deliberate harmful deception
  • Other: Any other violation not listed above

7. Blocking vs. Reporting

Blocking stops a user from contacting you. Reporting tells Kora about a policy violation. You can do both — block the user to protect yourself, and report them to help keep the Kora community safe.

8. Emergency Situations

If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services directly. Kora's reporting system is not a substitute for emergency services, and Kora cannot guarantee an immediate response to reports.

If you encounter child sexual exploitation or abuse, Kora will take immediate action and may report the matter to appropriate authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or equivalent authorities in your jurisdiction.

9. Privacy of Reports

Your identity as a reporter is kept confidential. Kora will not share your identity with the reported user. However, in rare cases where Kora is legally compelled to disclose reporter information (e.g., by court order), Kora may be required to comply.

Report data is handled according to Kora's Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

10. Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity and frequency of violations, Kora may take the following actions against reported users:

  • Content removal
  • Feature restrictions (e.g., limited messaging, limited calls)
  • Warnings
  • Temporary suspension
  • Permanent account disabling
  • Verification badge removal
  • Channel or group removal

Serious violations involving child safety, credible threats, or severe abuse may result in immediate permanent disabling without prior warning.

11. Appeals

If you believe Kora took incorrect enforcement action against your account, you may submit an appeal through the in-app appeal process or by contacting support@koramessenger.com. Kora will review the appeal and make a final decision.

12. Relationship to Other Policies

This Blocking and Reporting Policy works alongside Kora's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Privacy Policy, and AI Policy. Violations identified through reports are evaluated against all applicable policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

Kora may update this Blocking and Reporting Policy as the platform develops. The latest version will include the applicable Last Updated date.

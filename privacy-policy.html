8. Offline Voice Messages

Kora may allow you to record and send a voice message while your device has no network connection.

When this happens, Kora may temporarily store the recording on your device.

The message may display as pending until a network connection becomes available.

Once connectivity is restored, Kora may automatically upload and send the pending voice message.

Deleting the application, clearing application data, or removing locally stored information may prevent an unsent offline message from being delivered.

10. Voice and Video Consent

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary permission to upload another person's voice, audio, or video.

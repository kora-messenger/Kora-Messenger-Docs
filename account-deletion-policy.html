Account Deletion Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

At Kora Messenger, we believe you should have complete control over your personal data. In compliance with Apple App Store and Google Play Store developer requirements, this policy outlines how you can delete your account and what happens to your information when you do.

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your profile, premium features, and chat history cannot be recovered.

1. How to Delete Your Account

You can initiate an account deletion through two simple methods:

  • In-App Deletion (Recommended): Open Kora Messenger, navigate to Settings > Account > Delete Account, and confirm your choice. Your data will be wiped instantly.
  • Web Request (Without the App): If you have already uninstalled the app, you can request manual deletion by emailing our support team at koramessengersupport@gmail.com from your registered email address or phone records.

2. Data Removed Upon Deletion

When you delete your Kora Messenger account, the following data is permanently purged from our servers:

  • Profile Information: Your username, display name, registered phone number, and profile picture avatar.
  • Contact Relationships: Your app-specific friend lists and group chat memberships.
  • Premium Translation & Voice Profiles: Any synthesized biometric mathematical tokens created via our "Upload to Create a Voice" feature are entirely destroyed.
  • Message Delivery Queues: Any offline encrypted messages currently cached in our transit system awaiting delivery are permanently dropped.

3. Data Retention Exceptions

To keep our systems safe and satisfy global legal frameworks, minor exceptions apply:

  • Financial History: Your premium subscription purchase history is handled natively by Apple or Google. Kora does not own this transactional data, and you must review your app store account to clear transaction history.
  • Technical Server Logs: Anonymized routing telemetry and automated crash reports are retained temporarily (up to 90 days) for security diagnostics before permanent deletion.

4. Deletion Timeline

When you request account deletion via the in-app method, your data is purged immediately. For web-requested deletions, our support team processes the request within 72 hours. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

5. Re-Registration

After account deletion, you are free to create a new Kora Messenger account at any time using the same or different credentials. Previous data from your deleted account is not recoverable and will not be linked to your new account.

Having trouble deleting your account or need manual assistance? Contact our privacy team immediately at: koramessengersupport@gmail.com.
